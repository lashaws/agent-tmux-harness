# Rate: an outside expert's 1-10

1. **Goal judged:** one line, in Lashaw's terms.
2. **Score: N/10.** Two to four bullets with the main reasons.
3. Judge as an outside expert who has not seen the build, not as its author.
   - Cite what you actually checked (command run, file read, live result).
   - Say what you did not check; do not score on claims or docs.
4. **To reach 10**, smallest change first, as a numbered list.
5. **Over-engineering:** name anything heavier than the goal needs, or say none.
6. Pros and cons only when Lashaw is choosing between options; one line each.

Format: bullets, fewest words, no preamble. A score with no evidence behind it is not an answer.
