Agent can do it alone Needs Lashaw (sign-in or decision)

Start any of these now, in parallel.

List every report, its source tables and who reads it.

Pick between the two shortlisted options.

Unblocks: C, E

Each item waits on the Tier 1 items named in it.

CDesign the new schema

Needs: A, B   Unblocks: F

DSign in to the vendor console

Needs: A   Unblocks: E
